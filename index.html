Part 07

Asian Sub-Region Insights

Five sub-regions, five very different hydrocarbon stories. Each carries its own geology, fiscal regime and geopolitical risk.

🏙️ East Asia

China dominates, with mature onshore basins (Daqing, Shengli) and growing offshore and shale output. Japan and South Korea are major importers with limited domestic upstream.

Leader China Focus Onshore Trend Shale

🌴 Southeast Asia

Indonesia, Malaysia, Vietnam and Brunei anchor production. Offshore basins dominate; mature fields face decline while deepwater plays offer growth.

Leader Indonesia Focus Offshore Trend Deepwater

🕉️ South Asia

India is the key market, with growing refining capacity and exploration in the Krishna-Godavari and Rajasthan basins. Pakistan and Bangladesh have smaller but active fields.

Leader India Focus Onshore Trend Imports

🏔️ Central Asia

Kazakhstan and Turkmenistan hold vast Caspian reserves. Landlocked geography makes export routes — CPC, TAPI, China pipelines — a decisive strategic factor.

🕌 Middle East (Asia)

The Gulf states hold the world's lowest-cost reserves. Saudi Arabia, UAE, Iraq, Kuwait and Iran dominate global supply and are central to any energy transition scenario.

Part 08

Safety & Environment

Extraction is a high-consequence activity. These are the controls that make it manageable across Asian operating environments.

💥 Well Control

Blowout preventers, kick detection while drilling, and mud-weight management are non-negotiable. Every well must be able to be shut in within seconds.

💧 Water Protection

Surface casing plus cemented annulus isolates freshwater aquifers — critical in densely populated Asian river basins.

🌱 Emissions Reduction

Associated gas capture instead of flaring, methane leak detection, and electrified operations cut the carbon intensity of every barrel.

🛢️ Spill Response

Containment booms, skimmers and rapid shut-in valves. Pipeline integrity is monitored continuously — vital in the Strait of Malacca and South China Sea.

🌊 Monsoon & Typhoon Risk

Asian offshore operations face seasonal cyclones. Weather routing, evacuation plans and robust mooring design are essential.

♻️ Decommissioning

Wells are plugged with cement, platforms removed or reefed, and the site restored. The lifecycle does not end at the last barrel.
